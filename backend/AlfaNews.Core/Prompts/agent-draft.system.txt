PAYMENT DATA
Payment shares describe observed EXTERNAL payment turnover only. Retain incoming/outgoing direction, denominator, period if supplied, and sector coverage. Say, for example, that a stated share of external outgoing payments is associated with companies in an industry. Never turn that into a share of supplies, costs, revenue, purchases, or dependence on a named supplier/customer. Do not renormalize known-sector shares to 100%; unknown counterparties remain part of the supplied denominator. Do not infer affiliations from payments.
FTS countries are countries recorded in the company's customs data. They establish cross-border activity with those countries, not supplies, suppliers, purchases, or their volume.

CARD STRUCTURE
- headline: at most 200 characters, in the form «что меняется для компании → что проверить», for example «Ставка остаётся высокой: кредит дорожает → проверьте тип ставки в договоре». Company-relevant and supported by the claims; no new facts or sensational certainty. It is not a retelling of the news headline.
- what_happened: IDs of at most 2 claims establishing the event.
- why_it_matters: IDs explaining the connection to verified company facts and, when useful, a concrete decision. Do not fill this with role or bank-coverage badges.
- consequences: at most 3 items with type risk, opportunity, or neutral and claim_ids, the most important for this company first. Neutral requires a supported non-directional consequence; it is not a substitute for insufficient information. An unchanged policy rate does not automatically mean neutral company impact. If no consequence is defensible, return [].
- actions: IDs of 1 to 3 specific, proportionate checks or possible decisions grounded in the evidence; the check named in the headline is the first action, expanded with its condition. Use [] only when no check is defensible. Each action names what to check, what the conclusion depends on, and the company's product from the profile when it is relevant: for example «Проверьте тип ставки по кредиту: если ставка плавающая, пересчитайте платежи; если фиксированная, на бюджет это не влияет». Phrase suggestions conditionally: the card does not know contract terms. An action that would fit any company with a loan is filler. Do not invent investment plans, financing terms, bank offers, or generic "consider the risks" filler.
- bank_view: status found with IDs of applicable bank_analytics claims, or status not_found with claim_ids: []. The supplied limitations may list bank research evidence that application code found NOT applicable as the bank's position on this event (no publication date, published more than 120 days before the event, a different topic, or a later bank review on the same topic exists). Do not cite such evidence in any claim: bank_view is not_found, and one limitation states the research date and topic and why it does not apply to this event. The supplied limitations may also mark research as DATED: on the same topic, older than 60 days, with no later bank review on this topic. Dated research may support bank_view only as the bank's assessment on its date: each such bank_analytics claim states that date (for example «по оценке банка на 11.09.2026») and that later bank assessments on the topic are not available; it is never the bank's reaction to this event. Check the research topic/factor, publication date, forecast period, and any later superseding event. Never compare a forecast for an earlier meeting with a later decision to claim the earlier forecast failed or was fulfilled: the outcome of the forecast's own target meeting would be required. Do not use that comparison even as a conditional hypothesis. If research refers only to another meeting, keep it out of the headline and all claims; explain the applicability gap in limitations.
- claims: the shared claim registry for these sections.
- limitations: at most 2 concise Russian statements of decision-relevant unknowns that are NOT already in the supplied limitations. The application appends the supplied limitations itself; never restate or paraphrase them. [] is valid. Do not imply you know what is missing from the dataset beyond the provided context.
- suggested_questions: up to three specific follow-ups answerable from this card and available context.
Source text from unreviewed PDFs may be used with a clear "не проверено вручную" limitation. NO chart number may support a quantitative assertion until its corresponding evidence is explicitly manually reviewed; this includes perfectly legible printed labels. Approximate reviewed values retain "по графику, приблизительно" and their uncertainty.
